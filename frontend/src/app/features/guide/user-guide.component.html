How to Use MemoryVerse

A thoughtful guide to curating, organizing, and reliving the stories that define your collective history.

lightbulb The Philosophy
"MemoryVerse is not a cloud drive; it is your digital coffee-table book. It is designed to preserve the story behind the photo, not just the photo itself."

Traditional cloud storage dumps thousands of uncurated files into endless folders. MemoryVerse is built differently: every moment has an author, an emotion, a place in time, and an editorial narrative that companions can cherish for generations.

account_tree The Hierarchy

How to Organize Your Archive

Follow this four-tier structure to turn raw snapshots into cohesive, living chronicles:

01
auto_stories

Journeys

The Overarching Books

Start here. Journeys are the overarching "Books" of your life. They represent major chapters and shared eras (e.g., "B.Tech College Days", "Euro Trip 2025", "Our Startup Years").

tips_and_updates Create a Journey first before adding individual memories.
02
bookmark_border

Chapters

The Milestones & Eras

Inside Journeys, create Chapters for specific milestones, semesters, or trips (e.g., "First Year Hostel", "Paris Weekend", "Graduation Week").

tips_and_updates Chapters give your journey a rich chronological narrative spine.
03
photo_library

Memories

The Individual Pages

Memories are the individual "Pages" in the book. Create a memory, assign it to a Journey and Chapter, set the historical Date and Location, and write the story behind the moment.

tips_and_updates Focus on the emotional narrative—what made this specific day unforgettable.
04
collections

Media & Voice Notes

The Visuals & Audio

Finally, upload the high-resolution photographs, videos, and voice memos that belong to that specific memory. Listen to audio notes directly in the story.

tips_and_updates Supports JPG, PNG, WEBP, MP4 videos, and MP3/WAV audio notes.
